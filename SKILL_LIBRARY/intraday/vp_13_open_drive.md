## 🛠️ Setup Rules

### Entry Window
- **Only valid between 09:18 and 09:45** (first 30 minutes).

### Long Entry (Bullish OD)
1. First 3 candles of the day are **all green (bullish)**.
2. At least one of the 3 is a **Power Candle** (body >75% of range AND range >1.3× avg).
3. Entry = close of the 3rd candle (or any candle thereafter up to 09:45).
4. SL = lowest low of all 3 opening candles.

### Short Entry (Bearish OD)
1. First 3 candles of the day are **all red (bearish)**.
2. At least one is a Power Candle.
3. Entry = close. SL = highest high of all 3 opening candles.

### Target
- 2× risk distance.

## 💡 Notes
- OD = institutions decided direction before the open and execute aggressively from the first candle.
- **Never trade this on 2m** — too noisy, produces losing signals.
- 5m and 15m filter out the noise perfectly.
- When you see 3 consecutive power candles all in the same direction from market open — this is the highest conviction setup in the entire VP library.
- Wait patiently. This might only appear 1–2 times per week.
