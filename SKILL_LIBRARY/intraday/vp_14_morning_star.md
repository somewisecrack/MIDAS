## 🛠️ Setup Rules (3-Candle Pattern)
1. **Candle 1**: Large bearish (red) candle — strong selling.
2. **Candle 2**: Small body / Doji (body ratio < 30%) — indecision.
3. **Candle 3**: Large bullish (green) candle, body > 50% of Candle 1's body, **closes above EMA20**.
4. Entry = close of Candle 3. SL = lowest low of Candles 1 & 2.
5. Target = 2× risk.

## 💡 Notes
- Alone, this pattern has near-zero edge on automated testing.
- **Use as confirmation signal** when price is at: S1/S2 Pivot, Demand Zone (VP-23), or prior swing low.
- The bullish counterpart (Evening Star / VP-15) significantly outperforms this — NIFTY/BNF fall faster than they rise.
