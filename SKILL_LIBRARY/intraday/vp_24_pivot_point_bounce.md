## 🛠️ Setup Rules

### Pivot Levels Required
Calculate from **previous day's H, L, C**:
- **Pivot** = (H + L + C) / 3
- **R1** = 2×Pivot − L
- **S1** = 2×Pivot − H
- **R2** = Pivot + (H − L)
- **S2** = Pivot − (H − L)

### Long Entry (Bounce off Support Level)
1. Price approaches Pivot, S1, or S2 (within 0.1% of the level).
2. Current candle is **bullish** with **lower wick > body** (rejection of level).
3. Entry = close. SL = current candle low − 0.2%.
4. Target = 2× risk.

### Short Entry (Rejection at Resistance Level)
1. Price approaches Pivot, R1, or R2 (within 0.1% of the level).
2. Current candle is **bearish** with **upper wick > body** (rejection of level).
3. Entry = close. SL = current candle high + 0.2%.
4. Target = 2× risk.
