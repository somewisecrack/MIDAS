## 🛠️ Setup Rules

### Long Entry
1. In the last 10 bars, find a **bullish candle with lower wick > 2× its body** (master candle).
2. That long lower wick shows buyers rejected lower prices — institutions buying.
3. Current candle is **bullish** and closes **above the master candle's close**.
4. Price must be **above EMA20**.
5. Entry = current close. SL = master candle's low. Target = 2× risk.

### Short Entry
1. In the last 10 bars, find a **bearish candle with upper wick > 2× its body** (master candle).
2. Long upper wick shows sellers rejected higher prices.
3. Current candle is **bearish** and closes **below the master candle's close**.
4. Price must be **below EMA20**.
5. Entry = current close. SL = master candle's high. Target = 2× risk.

## 💡 Notes
- The wick candle = institutional order. Current candle closing through it = follow-through.
