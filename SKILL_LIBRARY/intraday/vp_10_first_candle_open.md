## 🛠️ Setup Rules

### Short Entry
1. Note the **open price of the first candle** at 09:15.
2. After 09:16, a **bearish Power Candle** closes **below that open price**.
3. Entry = close. SL = high of the entry candle.

### Long Entry
1. The first 09:15 candle must itself be **bullish** (strong opening drive).
2. After 09:16, a **bullish Power Candle** closes **above the first candle's open**.
3. Entry = close. SL = low of the entry candle.

**Power Candle**: Body > 75% of total range AND range > 1.3× 5-period average range.

### Target
- 2× risk distance.

## 💡 Notes
- The first candle's open acts as the "line in the sand" for the session.
- A strong move away from it signals institutional directional commitment.
- Avoid on NIFTY 15m (PF 0.59) — too few and too large moves per candle.
- Best traded within the first 45 minutes of the session.
