## 🛠️ Setup Rules

### Extreme Candle Definition
- Previous candle's range > **2.5× the 20-period average range** — institutionally outsized move.

### Long Entry (Fading a Bearish Extreme Candle)
1. Previous candle was a **bearish extreme candle** (range > 2.5× avg, red).
2. Current candle is **bullish** and closes **above the previous candle's close**.
3. Entry = close. SL = current candle's low.
4. Target = 2× risk.

### Short Entry (Fading a Bullish Extreme Candle)
1. Previous candle was a **bullish extreme candle** (range > 2.5× avg, green).
2. Current candle is **bearish** and closes **below the previous candle's close**.
3. Entry = close. SL = current candle's high.
4. Target = 2× risk.

## 💡 Notes
- Concept: A 2.5× range candle is a panic/euphoria move. The subsequent reversal candle signals the exhaustion of that extreme buying/selling pressure.
- **Only trade BANKNIFTY 15m for systematic execution.** NIFTY 15m is marginal; all 2m is losing.
- Low frequency (~17 trades / 58 days on BNF 15m) — a premium setup.
- On BANKNIFTY, these extreme candles often occur at major support/resistance, amplifying the reversal probability.
