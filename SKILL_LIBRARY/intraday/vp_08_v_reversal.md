## 🛠️ Setup Rules

### Entry Trigger (Long)
1. Price has made **5 or more consecutive bearish (red) candles** — exhaustion selling.
2. The **last red candle** has volume ≥ 80% of the average volume of prior red candles (capitulation).
3. A **green candle** forms and its High breaks above the last red candle's High.
4. Entry = break of last red candle's high. SL = lowest low of the entire down-move. Target = 2× risk.

## 💡 Notes
- 5+ consecutive red candles = panic exhaustion. The green breakout candle = buyers taking control.
- Volume check on the last red candle is critical — you want climactic volume (sellers exhausted), not declining volume (trend continuing).
- BANKNIFTY is ideal: higher volatility creates sharper V-shaped reversals.
- Rare setup (50–60 trades / 60 days) — wait for it.
