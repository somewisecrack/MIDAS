## 🛠️ Setup Rules

### Short Entry
1. A **bearish Power Candle** appeared in the last 10 bars (body >75% of range, large range).
2. Price retraces up and **touches the 50% level** of that red candle's body (midpoint of open-to-close).
3. Current candle is **bearish** and closes back below that 50% level.
4. Price must be **below EMA20**.
5. Entry = close. SL = high of the Power Candle.
6. Target = 2× risk.

## 💡 Notes
- Mirror of VP-16 (GCR). Institutions sold into the 50% pullback via a large red candle.
- The 50% level is where remaining sell orders are often placed.
- Best used with a Pivot resistance (R1/R2) or supply zone at the 50% level.
