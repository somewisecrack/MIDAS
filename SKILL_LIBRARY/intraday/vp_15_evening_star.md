## 🛠️ Setup Rules (3-Candle Pattern)
1. **Candle 1**: Large bullish (green) candle — strong buying.
2. **Candle 2**: Small body / Doji (body ratio < 30%) — exhaustion at top.
3. **Candle 3**: Large bearish (red) candle, body > 50% of Candle 1's body, **closes below EMA20**.
4. Entry = close of Candle 3. SL = highest high of Candles 1 & 2.
5. Target = 2× risk.

## 💡 Notes
- Dramatically outperforms its counterpart (Morning Star). NIFTY/BNF indices sell off more aggressively than they rally.
- Best at **resistance levels**: R1/R2 Pivots, supply zones, prior swing highs.
- On BANKNIFTY 2m — simply look for this pattern when price is near any significant resistance and enter short.
- Skip on 5m — mixed results, not worth the risk.
