## 🛠️ Setup Rules

### Pattern Detection (in last 20 bars)
1. Look for **two swing highs** at approximately the same price level (within 0.5% of each other).
2. The two peaks form the "M" shape — a classic Double Top.

### Entry Trigger (Short)
3. After the second peak, price breaks below the **neckline** (lowest low between the two peaks).
4. The neckline-breaking candle must be **bearish**.
5. Entry = close of the breakout candle.
6. SL = higher of the two peaks.
7. Target = 2× risk.

## 💡 Notes
- The M pattern is one of the most reliable reversal signals in technical analysis.
- Works on ALL timeframes — 2m, 5m, and 15m all show positive PF.
- Especially powerful on BANKNIFTY where the double tops often form near major resistance (R1, supply zones).
- Add confluence: look for an Evening Star (VP-15) completing the second top for very high probability.
