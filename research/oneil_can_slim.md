# Research: How to Make Money in Stocks (William O'Neil)

This document details the strategies and systems described in William O'Neil's masterpiece on growth stock investing.

## 1. The CAN SLIM System (The Fundamental Filter)
Winning stocks historically exhibit these 7 traits before their big moves:
- **C: Current Quarterly Earnings**: >25% growth (accelerating).
- **A: Annual Earnings**: >25% growth over last 3 years.
- **N: New Products/Management/Highs**: Catalysts that change perception and price.
- **S: Supply and Demand**: Low float, heavy volume buying by institutions.
- **L: Leader or Laggard**: Focus on top 2-3 stocks in an industry group (RS Rating >80).
- **I: Institutional Sponsorship**: At least a few institutional owners with recent increases.
- **M: Market Direction**: Only buy in "Confirmed Uptrends" (General Market M-Rating).

## 2. Technical Strategy Setups (The Bases)
O'Neil identifies these specific patterns for entry. Breakout must occur on **+40-50% volume**.

### [Setup 1] Cup with Handle
- **Structure**: Rounded "U" shape (not "V").
- **Depth**: 12-33% (up to 50% in bear markets).
- **Duration**: 7 to 65 weeks.
- **The Handle**: Must form in the upper half of the cup, drift downward on low volume. Buy on break of handle peak.

### [Setup 2] Double Bottom (W-Pattern)
- **Structure**: Second bottom must undercut the first bottom (shakes out weak hands).
- **Duration**: 7+ weeks.
- **Buy Point**: High of the middle peak of the "W".

### [Setup 3] Flat Base
- **Structure**: Moves sideways in a tight range (8-15% depth).
- **Duration**: 5+ weeks.
- **Context**: Occurs after a prior 20%+ advance from a Cup or Double Bottom.

### [Setup 4] Saucer with Handle
- **Structure**: Similar to Cup but much shallower and wider.
- **Duration**: Can last many months.

### [Setup 5] High Tight Flag (O'Neil Version)
- **Move**: 100%+ in 4 to 8 weeks.
- **Base**: Sideways consolidation of <25% depth.
- **Duration**: 3 to 5 weeks.
- **Buy Point**: Break of the flag high.

### [Setup 6] IPO Base
- **Structure**: First base formed after IPO.
- **Context**: Must happen within first 1-2 years of trading.

### [Setup 7] Base on Base
- **Structure**: A second base forms immediately on top of the first because the market turned.
- **Buy Point**: Breakout of the upper base.

### [Setup 8] Ascending Base
- **Structure**: Three pullbacks of 10-20% during a strong rally.
- **Buy Point**: Break of the 3rd pullback's peak.

## 3. Exit Strategies (The Sell Rules)
- **7-8% Stop Loss**: Automatic exit if the trade goes against you.
- **Climax Top**: 2-3 months of rally followed by vertical acceleration and "exhaustion" volume.
- **Distribution Days**: Heavy volume selling in the general market.
- **Breaking the 50-day SMA**: Warning sign of institutional exit.
