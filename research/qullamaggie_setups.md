# Qullamaggie: 3 Timeless Setups

Based on [My 3 timeless setups that have made me tens of millions](https://qullamaggie.com/my-3-timeless-setups-that-have-made-me-tens-of-millions/).

## 1. Breakouts (High Tight Flags / Holy Grail)

### The Setup
1. **Big Move**: A 30-100%+ move higher in the past 1-3 months (lasts a few days to weeks).
2. **Orderly Pullback**: Consolidation with higher lows and tightening range. Stock "surfs" the rising 10/20-day (sometimes 50-day) MA.
3. **Range Expansion**: Breakout out of consolidation (consolidation usually lasts 2 weeks to 2 months).

### How to Trade
- **Entry**: Enter on the **Opening Range High (ORH)**. Can be the high of the first 1-min, 5-min, or 60-min candle.
- **Stop Loss**: Always at the **Lows of the Day**. Stop should not be wider than the ATR or ADR (e.g., if ADR is 5%, stop is max 5%).
- **Exit/Take Profit**: 
    - Sell 1/3 to 1/2 after 3-5 days.
    - Move stop to break even.
    - Trail the rest using the 10-day or 20-day moving average (first close below).

## 2. Episodic Pivot (EP)

### The Setup
1. **Gap Up**: A gap up of 10%+ caused by unexpected news (Earnings, FDA, Guidance).
2. **Big Volume**: Needs massive volume (often full average daily volume traded in the first 15-30 mins).
3. **Growth Catalyst**: For earnings EPs, look for triple-digit EPS/Revenue growth and a significant analyst beat.
4. **Neglected Stock**: Best if the stock has *not* rallied in the previous 3-6 months.

### How to Trade
- **Entry**: Enter on the **Opening Range High (ORH)** (1m, 5m, or 60m).
- **Stop Loss**: At the **Lows of the Day**.
- **Exit/Take Profit**: Trail with the 10-day, 20-day, or 50-day moving average once they surpass the initial stop.

## 3. Parabolic Short (The Snapback)

### The Setup
1. **Stretched Price**: Stock up 50-100%+ in a few days (large cap) or 300-1000%+ (small cap).
2. **Succession of Green Days**: Up 3-5+ days in a row, accelerating away from moving averages.

### How to Trade
- **Entry**: Short on **Opening Range Lows (ORL)** (1m or 5m). 
    - Variation: Wait for the "first crack", a bounce back into **VWAP**, and enter on a VWAP fail (first red 1m/5m candle back through VWAP).
- **Stop Loss**: Highs of the Day or a reclaim of VWAP.
- **Target**: The **10-day or 20-day moving averages** (where stocks usually bounce).
- **Note**: Riskier than long setups; wait for the "amateur shorts" to get run over first. Long variations exist for stocks down 50-60%+ in a few days.
