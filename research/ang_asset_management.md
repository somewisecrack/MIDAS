# Research: Asset Management (Andrew Ang)

Andrew Ang's "Asset Management" is a seminal work on factor investing, shifting focus from asset classes to underlying factors.

## 1. Core Macro Factors
- **Economic Growth**: Exposure to the business cycle.
- **Inflation**: Protection against rising prices.
- **Liquidity**: Reward for holding illiquid assets.

## 2. Core Style Factors (The Audit Focus)
These are the systematic edges that can be backtested in the equity market:

### [Factor 1] Value
- **Concept**: Buying "cheap" stocks relative to fundamentals.
- **Metrics**: Low Price-to-Book (P/B), Price-to-Earnings (P/E), or Enterprise Value to EBITDA.
- **Audit Logic**: Long high-value (low ratio) stocks, potentially short low-value (high ratio) stocks.

### [Factor 2] Momentum
- **Concept**: Price trends tend to persist.
- **Metrics**: 12-month return (excluding the most recent month).
- **Audit Logic**: Buy the top decile of stocks sorted by 12-1 momentum.

### [Factor 3] Quality
- **Concept**: High-profitability stocks with low leverage.
- **Metrics**: Return on Equity (ROE), low debt-to-equity, stable earnings.
- **Audit Logic**: Rank stocks by a composite Quality score (ROE + Profitability + Low Leverage).

### [Factor 4] Low Volatility
- **Concept**: Low-risk stocks provide higher risk-adjusted returns.
- **Metrics**: Standard deviation of daily returns over the last 1-3 years.
- **Audit Logic**: Select the bottom quintile of stocks by historical volatility.

### [Factor 5] Size
- **Concept**: Small-cap stocks outperform large-cap stocks over long horizons.
- **Metrics**: Total Market Capitalization.
- **Audit Logic**: Long small-cap stocks, short large-cap stocks.

## 3. Systematic Strategy: Multi-Factor Tilting
- **Concept**: Combining factors to smooth out the cyclicality of individual factor performance.
- **Logic**: Equal-weight or risk-parity allocate to Value, Momentum, Quality, and Low Volatility.
