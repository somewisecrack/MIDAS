# Research: Price-Volume Analysis (Anna Coulling) - Full Strategy List

## 1. The Buying Climax
- **Setup**: Long bearish trend.
- **VPA**: Ultra-high volume on a hammer (long lower wick).
- **Signal**: Insiders absorbing panic selling; preparation for accumulation.

## 2. The Selling Climax
- **Setup**: Long bullish trend.
- **VPA**: Ultra-high volume on a shooting star (long upper wick).
- **Signal**: Insiders distributing into retail greed.

## 3. The Hanging Man
- **Setup**: Bullish trend peak.
- **VPA**: A hammer-shaped candle at a top with high volume.
- **Signal**: Hidden weakness (selling into the open, then marking it back up).

## 4. Stopping Volume
- **Setup**: Bearish trend.
- **VPA**: High volume on a candle with a narrow spread (compared to previous).
- **Signal**: The "brakes" being applied by professionals; accumulation beginning.

## 5. Topping Out Volume
- **Setup**: Bullish trend.
- **VPA**: High volume on a candle with a narrowing spread.
- **Signal**: Supply entering; distribution beginning.

## 6. No Demand Test
- **Setup**: Bullish trend pullback/consolidation.
- **VPA**: Low volume on a narrow-spread candle near the high.
- **Signal**: Confirmation that sellers are absent; trend ready to resume.

## 7. No Supply Test
- **Setup**: Bearish trend pullback.
- **VPA**: Low volume on a narrow-spread candle near the low.
- **Signal**: Confirmation of weakness; trend likely to continue lower.

## 8. The Trap Up Move
- **Setup**: Morning open or breakout.
- **VPA**: Gap up or rapid move higher on **Low Volume**.
- **Signal**: Insiders testing the air; likely a false breakout.

## 9. The Trap Down Move
- **Setup**: Morning open or breakout.
- **VPA**: Gap down on **Low Volume**.
- **Signal**: Testing the air lower; often a "shakeout" before a move higher.

## 10. Effort vs Result (Agreement)
- **VPA**: Wide Spread + High Volume (Strong move).

## 11. Effort vs Result (Anomaly)
- **VPA**: Narrow Spread + Ultra-High Volume (Reversal precursor).
- **VPA**: Wide Spread + Low Volume (Trap/Weakness).

## 12. Breakout Validation
- **VPA**: Breakout of consolidation MUST be accompanied by a significant volume surge.
- **Signal**: Low volume breakouts are high-probability failures.
