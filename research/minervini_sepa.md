# Research: Mark Minervini SEPA Methodology

This document details the Specific Entry Point Analysis (SEPA) methodology as described in "Trade Like a Stock Market Wizard" (2013).

## 1. The Trend Template (Stage 2 Uptrend)
A stock must meet all 8 criteria to be considered in a healthy Stage 2 uptrend:

1.  **Price > 150-day and 200-day MA**: The current stock price is above both the 150-day and 200-day moving averages.
2.  **150-day MA > 200-day MA**: The 150-day moving average is above the 200-day moving average.
3.  **Trending 200-day MA**: The 200-day moving average line is trending up for at least 1 month (preferably 4–5 months or more).
4.  **50-day MA > 150-day and 200-day MA**: The 50-day moving average is above both the 150-day and 200-day moving averages.
5.  **Current Price > 25% above 52-week Low**: The many of the best performers are often 100%, 300%, or more above their low before they emerge from a consolidation.
6.  **Current Price within 25% of 52-week High**: The closer to a new high, the better.
7.  **Relative Strength (RS) Ranking > 70**: Preferably in the 80s or 90s. (RS Line should also be trending up).
8.  **Price > 50-day MA**: As the stock is coming out of a base, the price should be above the 50-day moving average.

## 2. VCP (Volatility Contraction Pattern)
The VCP is the technical "footprint" of institutional accumulation.

### Characteristics:
-   **Contractions (T)**: Each successive pullback (from left to right) becomes shallower and shorter in duration.
-   **Tightening Supply**: Volume should contract significantly on the right side of the base.
-   **The Pivot Point**: A "cheat" or "handle" area where price moves sideways very tightly (e.g., < 2-3% range) before the breakout.
-   **Time**: Bases can last from 3 weeks to many months.

## 3. SEPA Fundamentals
Minervini's "Superperformance" stocks often show:
-   **Triple Digit EPS Growth**: At least 1-2 quarters of 100%+ earnings growth.
-   **Sales Growth**: Acceleration in revenue growth.
-   **Margin Expansion**: Improving profit margins.
-   **ROE**: High and improving Return on Equity.

## 4. Backtest Strategy
1.  **Filter**: Apply Trend Template to the universe of stocks.
2.  **Setup Detection**: Identify consolidations within Stage 2.
3.  **VCP Logic**: Check for decreasing volatility (standard deviation of daily returns) within the consolidation.
4.  **Entry**: Buy on volume-backed breakout of the pivot point.
5.  **Risk**: 7-8% hard stop; trail with moving averages or a "climax top" signal.
