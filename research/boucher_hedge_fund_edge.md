# Research: The Hedge Fund Edge (Mark Boucher)

Mark Boucher's methodology focuses on "Runaway Trends" and a top-down global approach to maximize reward while strictly controlling risk.

## 1. Runaway Momentum Strategy
This is the primary technical strategy for individual stocks.
- **Goal**: Identify stocks that are about to go "vertical" (50-100% moves in weeks).
- **The "TBBLBG" Entry Logic**: 
    - **Thrust Breakout**: Strong price move through a level.
    - **Breakout Lap**: Opening above the previous day's high/range.
    - **Breakaway Gap**: Gapping above a consolidation level.
- **Classic Flag Setup**:
    - **Extension**: A steep price run-up.
    - **Consolidation**: A tight sideways flag/base correction (ideally <26% or 38% depth).
    - **Duration**: At least 4 weeks (21 bars) of consolidation.
    - **Trigger**: Breakout to new highs via gap/lap/thrust.

## 2. Global Trend Trading (The Top-Down Filter)
Boucher doesn't just look at charts; he filters for current market leaders.
- **RS Ranking**: Use Relative Strength tables to find the top countries, top sectors, and then top stocks.
- **New High List Concentration**: Watch for a specific sector dominating the "New Highs" list—this signals a secular theme.
- **Austrian Liquidity Cycle**: Economic filters (interest rates, liquidity) to determine if it's "safe" to be fully invested.

## 3. Position Management & Risk
- **2% Portfolio Risk**: Maximum risk on any single trade.
- **Boucher Stop**: Technical chart-based stops. If no support is nearby, exit on a 40% correction of the runaway move.
- **Paring Down**: Selling partial positions every 21-bar (1 month) period to reduce risk as the trend matures.

## 4. Fundamental Overlay
- **Earnings Growth**: Look for explosive earnings growth to confirm secular trends.
- **Valuation**: Uses basic valuation indexes to avoid "bubble" extremes.
