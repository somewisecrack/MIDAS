# Research: Street Smarts (Raschke & Connors) - Full Audit

## 1. Turtle Soup (20-Day Reversal)
- **Setup**: New 20-day low. Previous 20-day low was at least 4 sessions ago.
- **Entry**: Buy stop 5-10 ticks above the *previous* 20-day low.
- **Exit**: Trailing stop. Day trade or 2-3 day swing.

## 2. Turtle Soup Plus One
- **Setup**: Close today at a new 20-day extreme.
- **Entry**: Next day, entry stop at the *previous* 20-day extreme.

## 3. 80-20's (Range Reversal)
- **Setup**: Open in top 20% of daily range, Close in bottom 20% (or vice-versa).
- **Entry**: Next day, trade 5-10 ticks past yesterday's low/high, then reverse and enter at yesterday's extreme.
- **Exit**: Day trade.

## 4. Momentum Pinball
- **Setup**: Daily LBR/RSI < 30 (LBR/RSI = RSI(3, Close - Close[1])).
- **Entry**: Next day, buy stop above 1st hour High.
- **Exit**: Day 3 morning (2nd day after setup).

## 5. The Anti
- **Setup**: Uses 7-period %K and 10-period %D. %D has a clear slope.
- **Entry**: %K hooks in the direction of %D.
- **Exit**: 2-4 day swing.

## 6. The Holy Grail
- **Setup**: ADX(14) > 30 and rising.
- **Retracement**: Price touches 20-EMA.
- **Entry**: Buy stop above the high of the touch bar.
- **Exit**: Recent swing high.

## 7. Spike and Ledge
- **Setup**: Vertical price spike followed by a "ledge" (narrow congestion of 4-10 bars).
- **Entry**: Breakout of the ledge in the direction of the spike (or reversal).

## 8. Whirlpool
- **Setup**: Price/EMA interaction (similar to graveyard/pullback setups).

## 9. Three Little Indians
- **Setup**: Three symmetrical, converging peaks/valleys at a trend climax.
